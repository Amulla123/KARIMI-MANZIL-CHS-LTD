About Our Society

Karimi Manzil Co-operative Housing Society Ltd is formed and registered under the Maharashtra Co-operative Housing Society Act, 1961, and is classified as a Tenant Co-Partnership Housing Society (भाडेकरू सह-भागीदारी गृहनिर्माण संस्था).

The Building

Redevelopment Status

The building has completed over twenty years of age, with visible wall cracks and ceiling plaster damage in several units. At a Special General Body Meeting held on 15th July 2026, members resolved to proceed with redevelopment and to appoint a qualified architect for the project.
